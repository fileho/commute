Live data: PID GTFS-realtime feed (api.golemio.cz), about 4 MB per refresh.
How it predicts: from where the bus is now (GPS), it adds up how long each remaining stop-to-stop stretch took in the last 20 minutes. That uses every 58 and every bus line sharing the stretch (136, 201, 195, 110 and others), and extends each stretch's trend to when the bus gets there. A trolleybus standing still between stops, or overdue at its next stop, counts as held up. One that hasn't left the terminus yet can't leave before its previous trip ends plus a 2-minute turnaround. The leave time uses the early end of the range, so you're less likely to miss the bus.
